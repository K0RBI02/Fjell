## 1.2

The first release as **Fjell**, now open source under the GPL-3.0.

### New
- Your own wallpapers: pick a picture, drag a line to where the calm area for your apps begins, done.
  Clock and media colors adapt to the picture
- Three built-in scenes drawn in code: Fjord, Dusk and Winter
- The wallpaper fades over when the scene changes
- Timetable slots can follow sunrise and sunset (computed on the phone from the coarse location)
- All-day events (birthdays, holidays) show on the clock line until 6 pm

### Improved
- Relevant row: just installed apps, then the most used, then apps from the last two days, then the rest
- Apps an icon pack doesn't cover keep their original icon
- New notifications show as a hairline arc around the icon's top-right corner, in the icon's own color
- Long icon pack names wrap instead of being cut off
- The winter scene can be any wallpaper, including your own

## 1.1

### New
- Wallpaper timetable: pick which scene shows from which time, plus optional snow all winter
- Next calendar event (within 4 hours) can take the alarm's spot on the clock line
- Small music wave next to the track title
- Wallpaper previews in the settings show the scene's motif

### Improved
- Long track titles scroll like a car radio, with soft edges
- Tapping the alarm opens that exact alarm in the clock app
- Weather refreshes when the screen turns on and every 30 minutes on the home screen
- Signed with a dedicated release key

### Fixed
- A few clouds at night showed the sun instead of the moon
- The search bar could reopen a page left open in the Google app instead of a fresh search
- Search lists names that start with what you typed first ("ma" → Maps, Maces before Amazon)
- Simple icons crashed on Android 10–12

## 1.0

The first release: a calm home screen with a hand-lettered clock and flat landscape scenes.

- Hand-lettered clock with date, weather and next alarm
- 4×3 app grid in the calm band of the wallpaper, with drag and drop and an optional search bar
- The stack: every app A–Z with letter headers, an alphabet strip and a relevant row
- Search across apps, calculations, contacts (with a direct call button) and the web
- Icon packs (Nothing by default), grayscale, simple and custom icons, globally or per app
- Now-playing line with play/pause and skip; notification dots; app shortcuts
- Double tap to lock, swipe down for notifications, automatic scene by time and season
